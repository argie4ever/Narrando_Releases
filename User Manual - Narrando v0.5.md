## 1. The Narrando Philosophy: BEING, BECOMING, and MANUSCRIPT

Most writing software forces authors into an artificial compromise: either a rigid text document where structural perspective is lost, or a disconnected board of index cards where literary cadence dissolves.

**Narrando** eliminates this friction through a creative trinity powered by the **Soft Sync** paradigm:

* **🌌 BEING (Domain of Existence — Ontology & Lore):** The timeless conceptual map of your narrative universe. Here reside characters, locations, key props, factions, and dynamic relationships.
* **⚡ BECOMING (Domain of Sequence — 2D Causal Board):** The temporal canvas where events unfold chronologically. It governs cause-and-effect linkages, step outlinings, Act and Sequence structures, and the dramatic tension curve.
* **✍️ MANUSCRIPT (Domain of Writing — 1D Screenplay Canvas):** Continuous literary composition in industry-standard typography. Write dialogue, action lines, and scene headings with total focus.

> 📌 **Note — The Soft Sync Principle:** What you write in the Manuscript seamlessly informs the Outline; what you reorganize on the Causal Board instantly reorders the Manuscript; and entities created in the world are immediately available for intelligent autocompletion as you type. Neither layer gets in the way of the other.

### 1.1. BEING: The Domain of Existence (Ontology & Worldbuilding)
The domain of **BEING** is the conceptual map of your universe, free from chronological constraints. This is where your characters, locations, key objects, factions, and relationship networks live.
* In this space, entities exist on their own terms and interconnect via logical relationships (family ties, hierarchies, geography, alliances, or rivalry).
* It allows you to design your project's "Story Bible" before, during, or after plotting the storyline.

### 1.2. BECOMING: The Domain of Temporal Sequence & Causality
The domain of **BECOMING** is where events occur across time.
* It governs causal progression: how an inciting incident in scene one triggers a crisis at the midpoint and resolves in the climax.
* It is the territory of the **step outline**, distribution across **Acts and Sequences**, and the modulation of the **Dramatic Tension Curve**.
* Every atomic unit here is an **Action**: the minimal unit of dramatic transformation.

### 1.3. MANUSCRIPT: The Domain of Continuous Literary Writing
The **MANUSCRIPT** is the canvas where the story is read and experienced as a finished piece of cinematic writing.
* It provides a distraction-free writing environment formatted in industry-standard typography (Courier Prime).
* It enables you to focus on the pacing of action, subtext in dialogue, and atmosphere, while the underlying causal engine preserves background structure.

## 5. The Causal Board (BECOMING)

The **Causal Board (BECOMING)** is an infinite 2D canvas where your story's events are plotted, linked, and visualized. Here, narrative structure behaves as an elastic, relational network.

Every card on the board represents a **Dramatic Action**, displaying its scene number, synoptic body text, **Dramatic Tension Score (0 to 100)**, and its **Sandbox Toggle (⏸)**. Cards connect via directional causal lines that trace the story's consequence chains.

### 5.1. The Atomic Unit: The Action
An **Action** is the minimal unit of dramatic transformation in Narrando. It is not merely a block of text, but an event with dramatic weight, estimated duration, and ripple effects across the plot.

Three intuitive ways to create an Action:
1. **Top Bar "+ Action" Button:** Appends a new action at the first available chronological slot in the step outline.
2. **Context Menu (Right-click anywhere on the canvas):**
   * **Create in Sandbox:** Generates a free-floating action, perfect for capturing raw ideas that do not yet have a defined chronological place.
   * **Create Ordered:** Inserts an action directly integrated into the timeline sequence at that exact spatial coordinate.
3. **Keyboard Shortcut (`Ctrl + N`):** Instantly summons a new action subordinate to the active scene.

### 5.2. Sandbox Mode (Shelved Actions & Variations)
Each action card features a **Sandbox Button (⏸)** in its header. Toggling it:
* Detaches the action into an "unbound / paused" state: it drops out of the chronological sequence, disappears from the Timeline Tension Curve, and does not add to estimated script runtime.
* **Sandbox Use Cases:**
  - Preserving alternate dialogue, endings, or jokes without deleting them.
  - Brainstorming tentative scene ideas during writers' room sessions.
  - Holding B-plots and subplots in reserve until they mature.
* **Reintegration:** To restore an action from the Sandbox into the active narrative, click **Sandbox** again; the system re-indexes it into the chronological sequence based on its board coordinate.

### 5.3. The Law of Natural Expansion
To prevent visual clutter and card collisions as stories expand to hundreds of scenes, Narrando’s spatial physics engine enforces the **Law of Natural Expansion**:
* When you drag or expand an action card vertically within a column, the engine detects whether it would collide with cards below it.
* If a potential overlap is detected, it **smoothly pushes down** all actions and groups positioned below that Y-coordinate.
* This push is strictly downward (positive Y-axis) and cascading, ensuring that your layout never overlaps or collapses, always preserving clean breathing room for new beats.

### 5.4. Causal Links & Plot Relationships
On the Causal Board, narrative causality is established by dragging connector lines from the circular handles on card edges:
* **Drawing Edges:** Connect Card A to Card B to indicate that Event A causally triggers Event B.
* **Floating Edge Inspector:** Clicking any connector line opens a draggable popover where you can:
  - Assign a descriptive label (e.g., *"Trigger"*, *"Direct Consequence"*, *"Dramatic Irony"*, *"Parallel Subplot"*).
  - Change the edge color to visually color-code distinct character arcs or plotlines.
  - Sever the causal link without affecting either connected card.

### 5.5. Scene Groups & Multi-Selection
Selecting multiple cards (via marquee selection or `Shift + Click`) and right-clicking unlocks collective tools:
* **Group:** Wraps the selected actions inside a tinted container frame with an editable title. Moving the group container shifts all child actions as a single unit.
* **Color Categorization:** Simultaneously applies one of the 8 project palette colors across all selected cards.
* **Unified Deletion:** Choose between *Ungroup* (remove the bounding frame while preserving all actions in place) or *Delete* (remove the entire block from the project).

## 6. The Ontology Canvas (BEING)

The domain of **BEING** is the space dedicated to worldbuilding, relational mapping, and the underlying mythology of your story.

On this canvas, universe components (characters, locations, key props, factions, and concepts) are represented as interactive nodes connected by logical and thematic relationships.

### 6.1. Entities & Archetypes
In the Ontology Canvas, world elements are categorized across 5 primary archetypes:
* **👤 Characters:** Protagonists, antagonists, supporting cast, and background figures.
* **📍 Locations:** Primary sets, fictional realms, interiors, and exteriors.
* **📦 Objects:** Key props, *MacGuffins*, weapons, documents, and relics.
* **🏛️ Factions:** Organizations, families, corporations, or clans.
* **🏷️ Concepts / Tags:** Thematic motifs, emotional arcs, or symbolic codes.

### 6.2. The Immersive Entity Sheet
Double-clicking any entity (on the canvas or in the side library) opens its comprehensive **Entity Sheet**:
* **Polymorphic Section Titles:** The descriptor header dynamically adapts its terminology based on archetype (*"Biography"* for Characters, *"Environment Description"* for Locations, *"Physical Properties"* for Objects).
* **Aliases & Nicknames:** Register alternate monikers (e.g., *"The Stranger"*, *"Doc"*, *"Central Precinct"*) that the system leverages for autocompletion and automated text extraction.
* **Custom Attributes:** Dynamic key/value pairs to track age, profession, motivations, flaws, or coordinates.
* **Cross-Reference Appearance Log:** Displays a complete list of every scene where the entity appears. If a scene lacks a title, the engine generates an intelligent reference snippet based on its opening action.

### 6.3. Spatial Hyper-Navigation
Narrando eliminates the friction between world design and drafting:
* Clicking any scene listed under the **Appearances** section of an Entity Sheet closes the modal and **smoothly flies the camera** to center and focus that exact scene on the Causal Board or in the Manuscript.
* If you are in pure Ontology view, the interface automatically switches to **DUAL** mode so you can view the scene in time without losing your world context.

### 6.4. Entity Merging (Duplicate Resolution)
If duplicate entities were created during fast writing or imports (e.g., *"Viktor"* and *"Viktor Petrov"*):
1. Select the duplicate entities in the library or canvas and click **Merge Entities**.
2. Designate the **Primary Entity**, which retains its canonical name, archetype, and biography.
3. The system re-routes all dialogue, appearances, and causal links from the secondary entities to the primary entity, cleanly deleting duplicates without data loss.

### 6.5. Color Taxonomy Manager
Accessible from the BEING toolbar, the **Color Tag Manager** lets you rename and customize the narrative meaning of the system's 8 palette colors (e.g., assigning Blue to *"Romantic Subplot"*, Red to *"High Danger / Climax"*, or Green to *"Northern Faction"*).

## 7. The Manuscript Canvas

The **Manuscript Canvas** is Narrando's literary core: a continuous writing environment where your screenplay unfolds as an industry-standard script with real-time formatting.

At the top of the editor, a floating toolbar houses focus tools: the scene numbering toggle (`# Scenes`), the **Lexical Focus** selector, the **Cast Focus** dropdown, and a live counter of total scenes and actions.

### 7.1. Cinematic Typography & Industry Standards
The manuscript adheres strictly to international film industry standards (monospaced Courier Prime). Scene headings, action lines, character names, parentheticals, dialogue blocks, and transitions are formatted with automatic indentation and margins.

### 7.2. Intelligent Autocompletion (IntelliSense)
As you write, Narrando accelerates your typing speed without requiring mouse interaction:
* **Slugline Prefixes:** Typing the first letters of a line brings up standard prefixes (`INT.`, `EXT.`, `INT/EXT.`, `EST.`). Pressing **Tab** completes the slugline immediately.
* **Time-of-Day Tokens:** Typing a hyphen after a location prompts canonical time tokens (`DAY`, `NIGHT`, `DAWN`, `DUSK`, `CONTINUOUS`).
* **Silent Vocabulary Ingestion:** If you type an unconventional time token (e.g., *"GOLDEN HOUR"*, *"MIDNIGHT"*), the system silently registers it into the Project Dictionary for future autocomplete suggestions.
* **Character Dialogue:** Typing a character's name in uppercase triggers instant autocomplete; pressing **Enter** centers the cursor for immediate dialogue input.

### 7.3. Lexical Focus (Structural Reading Modes)
The **Lexical Focus** selector in the Manuscript toolbar lets you isolate specific storytelling layers:
* **Full Document:** Standard complete view of the screenplay.
* **Dialogue Only:** Hides all action lines, allowing writers to audit dialogue flow, voice distinctiveness, and conversational cadence in one pass.
* **Action Only:** Hides dialogue to evaluate visual rhythm, physical choreography, and cinematic imagery.
* **Sluglines Only:** Displays only scene headings and times (skeleton outline view).

### 7.4. Cast Focus (Actor Isolation Mode)
The **Cast Focus** menu allows you to isolate a specific character (e.g., *"JOHN"*).
* The manuscript dims or filters out scenes where the selected character does not appear, bringing their specific lines and actions into sharp focus.
* Indispensable for table reads, actor rehearsals, and character arc consistency audits.

### 7.5. Floating Bubble Menu (`+ Entity` on the Fly)
Selecting any text snippet in the manuscript (such as a new prop like *"THE ANTIQUE POCKET WATCH"* or an unlisted character) summons a floating menu with the **+ Entity** button. Clicking it opens the entity creation card to catalog the asset in the BEING domain without leaving your manuscript.

## 8. Artificial Intelligence Assistants

Narrando incorporates natural language processing assistants engineered not to generate generic placeholder text, but to serve as **rhythm and pacing auditors and mechanical workflow accelerators**.

### 8.1. Script Doctor (Dramatic Tension Audit)
The **Script Doctor** audits the dramatic progression of the entire screenplay. When triggered via the **🩺** button on the top toolbar:
* It reads the chronological sequence of actions and calculates a conflict and emotional intensity score (0 to 100) for each.
* It plots the **Dramatic Tension Curve** across the Timeline.
* It produces a structured qualitative report highlighting pacing valleys, flat stretches, and climax intensity.
* **Live Progress Streaming & Full Control:** A progress window displays scene-by-scene analysis. You can click **Cancel** at any time; the interface recovers instantly while retaining all analysis completed up to that point.

### 8.2. Assisted Scene Breakdown
Selecting a dense block of prose or an imported scene and clicking **Break Down**:
* The AI fragments long paragraphs into atomic action cards based on transformation verbs.
* Choose between three distinct modes:
  - **Structural Mode:** Generates a high-level summary ideal for outline treatments.
  - **Atomic Mode:** Maximum granularity into atomic actions (one transformation per card).
  - **Custom Mode:** Allows you to inject specific instructions to guide the breakdown.

### 8.3. Mass Entity Extraction
When importing existing screenplays or prose treatments, the extraction assistant scans the entire text to identify characters, locations, and key objects:
* **Match Preview:** Displays a clear occurrence count for each suggested asset.
* **Intelligent Global Assignment:** Automatically links confirmed characters across every card where they are mentioned, normalizing casing, abbreviations, and accents.

### 8.4. Multi-Provider Configuration & Privacy
From the **AI Settings** panel:
* **Supported Providers:** Native connectors for Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, and 100% private, offline local models via **Ollama**.
* **Security:** API keys are encrypted locally on your device and are never transmitted to third-party tracking services.

## 9. Machina Analytica & Dramatic Pacing Metrics

The **Machina Analytica (📊)** panel delivers a comprehensive quantitative x-ray of your story without interrupting your creative momentum.

| Analytical Dimension | Key Indicators | Practical Utility for Writers & Producers |
|---|---|---|
| **Script Volume** | Total actions, word count, estimated runtime. | Control pacing, page count, and runtime targets. |
| **Environmental Balance** | Interior vs. Exterior percentage. | Evaluate visual variety and set budget allocations. |
| **Temporal Balance** | Day vs. Night scene distribution. | Optimize shooting schedules and lighting department logistics. |
| **Conflict Curve** | Average tension, peak climaxes, and valleys. | Diagnose narrative drive and identify second-act slowdowns. |
| **Cast Density** | Global presence, relative category share, focus ratio. | Measure character screen time and balance ensemble casts. |

### 9.1. The Continuous Tension Curve (Timeline)
Located at the bottom of the workspace, this interactive chart plots story conflict over continuous coordinates:
* **X-Axis (Horizontal):** The chronological sequence of the screenplay.
* **Y-Axis (Vertical):** Dramatic intensity (0 to 100).
* **Act & Sequence Bands:** Visual markers delineating major story blocks. Dragging the outer edges of any Act or Sequence band elastically resizes its scope, automatically reassigning the scenes within its boundaries.

### 9.2. Heatmap Mode (Thermographic View)
Activating the **🔥** icon tints action cards on the Causal Board with a gradient corresponding to their tension score (from cool greens for decompressed scenes to fiery reds for climax peaks). Spot rhythm issues by simply zooming out on the board.

### 9.3. Density & Impact Metrics
Calculates the exact weight of each narrative asset:
* **Global Presence:** Percentage of scenes in which an entity participates relative to the entire script.
* **Relative Share:** Protagonism percentage within its own archetype (e.g., a supporting character's screen time relative to other supporting cast members).
* **Specific Focus:** Dominance ratio of the entity in scenes where it is actively present.

## 13. Workflow 1: Creative Genesis (From Blank Canvas to Finished Script)

> **Pipeline:** `1. Sandbox Brainstorming ➔ 2. 2D Causal Step Outline ➔ 3. DUAL Mode Worldbuilding ➔ 4. Fountain Manuscript ➔ 5. Script Doctoring ➔ 6. Final FDX/PDF Export`

### Objective:
Build a screenplay from scratch, moving naturally from raw initial brainstorming to a finalized script ready for production.

### Step-by-Step Guide:
1. **Sandbox Brainstorming:**
   - Create a new project and switch to the **Causal Board (BECOMING)**.
   - Spawn multiple actions using **Create in Sandbox**. Jot down ideas, plot twists, and key scene concepts without worrying about chronological order.
2. **Causal Plotting & Sequencing:**
   - Toggle off Sandbox mode on actions as they find their place in the narrative.
   - Draw causal connector lines to trace how early events trigger subsequent crises.
   - Create **Acts and Sequences** on the bottom Timeline to establish classic 3-Act or 5-Act story momentum.
3. **Worldbuilding in DUAL Mode:**
   - Switch to **DUAL** view. On the bottom canvas (Ontology), build character sheets and locations as they emerge from the step outline.
4. **Drafting in the Manuscript:**
   - Switch to **MANUSCRIPT** view. In the left Quad-Dock, activate the **Linear Editor** widget to navigate smoothly between scenes.
   - Draft scenes using *IntelliSense* autocomplete to jump seamlessly between sluglines, characters, and dialogue.
5. **Dramatic Tension Audit with Script Doctor:**
   - Run the **Script Doctor (🩺)**. Inspect the Tension Curve to verify that the climax hits target intensity and that the second act maintains drive.
6. **Professional Delivery:**
   - Open the Export Wizard, select **Screenplay**, enable the **Professional Title Page**, and generate your **.fdx** and **.pdf** files.

### Objective:
Transform an existing visual mind map or node network from Obsidian or Heptabase into a structured story workspace.

### Step-by-Step Guide:
1. **Direct Drag-and-Drop Ingestion:**
   - Drag your `.canvas` file from your desktop directly onto Narrando's window.
2. **Select Routing Strategy:**
   - Choose **Intelligent Hybrid Ingestion** to direct character/location nodes to **BEING** and chronological event cards to **BECOMING**.
3. **Ontology Consolidation:**
   - Switch to **BEING** view. Assign archetypes to imported entities and use **Merge Entities** to unify duplicate entries.
4. **Step Outline Organization:**
   - Switch to **BECOMING** view. Group loose actions into scenes and establish Sequence boundaries.
5. **Story Bible Delivery:**
   - Export a **Markdown Wiki with relational diagrams** to document your universe before drafting dialogue.

## 15. Workflow 3: Literary Adaptation (From Prose / Novel to Screenplay)

> **Pipeline:** `1. Prose Ingestion ➔ 2. AI Atomic Breakdown ➔ 3. Entity Extraction ➔ 4. Drafting with Fossil ➔ 5. Step Outline & Script`

### Objective:
Adapt a full novel, short story, or prose treatment into an industry-standard screenplay.

### Step-by-Step Guide:
1. **Prose File Ingestion:**
   - Drag your `.docx`, `.pdf`, or `.txt` file into Narrando and select the **Prose (Master Container)** strategy.
2. **AI Atomic Breakdown:**
   - Select book chapters and click **Break Down (✨)** in atomic mode. The AI fragments the narrative into distinct dramatic actions.
3. **Global Entity Extraction:**
   - Run **Entity Extraction with Global Assignment** to catalog all mentioned characters and link them across their respective scenes.
4. **Live Cross-Referencing with Fossil (Original Text):**
   - While drafting dialogue in the Manuscript, load the **Original Text (Fossil)** widget in the Quad-Dock to view the original prose side-by-side in read-only mode.
5. **Adaptation Step Outline Export:**
   - Export an **Outline Treatment in PDF** to review the adaptation structure with rights holders and producers.

## 16. Workflow 4: Dramatic Consulting & Script Doctoring (Audit & Rewrite)

> **Pipeline:** `1. FDX / Fountain Ingestion ➔ 2. AI Tension Diagnostics ➔ 3. Lexical & Cast Focus ➔ 4. Surgical Rewrite ➔ 5. Executive Dossier`

### Objective:
Evaluate dramatic rhythm, emotional pacing, and character consistency in a finished script during rewrite phases.

### Step-by-Step Guide:
1. **Zero-Friction Script Import:**
   - Drag your `.fdx` or `.fountain` file into Narrando. The script is instantly loaded into active memory.
2. **Run Script Doctor Audit:**
   - Launch the audit. The system scores the tension of every action and outputs a qualitative diagnostic breakdown.
3. **Identify Valleys in Heatmap & Timeline:**
   - Toggle **Heatmap Mode (🔥)** on the board to spot low-conflict flat stretches (coded in cool greens/blues) that require heightened stakes.
4. **Focused Rewriting:**
   - In the Manuscript, toggle **Lexical Focus (Dialogue Only)** to audit dialogue subtext.
   - Use **Cast Focus** to track the antagonist's trajectory exclusively and verify motive consistency.
   - Leave revision notes in the **Marginalia** widget for each scene.
5. **Diagnostic Delivery:**
   - Export an **Executive Analytics Dossier** with tension graphs and recommendations for the production team.

   - Review the **Workload View** to audit Day/Night ratios and total pages/eighths per scene.

   - Download the **Breakdown Sheet in CSV** and the **Relational ZIP Package** for distribution to department heads.

## B. Canonical Narrando Glossary

* **Action:** The atomic unit of dramatic transformation, causality, and storytelling weight.
* **BECOMING:** The domain of time, chronological sequence, causal step outlining, and tension modulation.
* **BEING:** The domain of ontology, worldbuilding, character lore, and relational networks.
* **Cast Focus:** Selective display filter in the Manuscript that isolates the dialogue lines and scenes of a specific character.
* **Day-out-of-Days (DOOD):** Production scheduling matrix auditing work, hold, and release days for every cast member.
* **Entity:** Any persistent narrative asset within the story universe (Character, Location, Object, Faction, or Concept).
* **Fossil (Original Text):** Quad-Dock widget preserving read-only access to source prose for adaptation comparisons.
* **Heatmap (Thermographic Mode):** Board visualization overlay tinting cards according to their dramatic conflict score (cool green to fiery red).
* **IntelliSense:** Real-time contextual autocompletion engine for sluglines, time tokens, and character names.
* **Law of Natural Expansion:** Spatial physics rule that smoothly pushes lower elements down when cards expand or move, preventing collisions.
* **Lexical Focus:** Structural filter in the Manuscript to inspect Dialogue Only, Action Only, or Sluglines Only.
* **MANUSCRIPT:** Continuous literary screenwriting canvas formatted in Courier Prime typography.
* **Marginalia:** Scratchpad system for timeless, acausal notes attached to scenes without altering screenplay duration.
* **Quad-Dock:** Modular 4-quadrant panel architecture allowing users to dock up to 8 specialized widgets.
* **Sandbox (Boneyard):** Holding bay where action cards float freely without affecting chronological order or the tension curve.
* **Script Doctor:** Artificial intelligence assistant that audits screenplay tension and flags pacing irregularities.
* **Soft Sync:** Architectural paradigm ensuring real-time harmony between 2D spatial freedom and 1D literary sequence.
* **Time Machine (Vault):** Historical repository of self-contained project snapshots for non-destructive version control and instant rollback.
